## What sponsorships fund

- Device testing (including HyperOS / Xiaomi quirks)
- Continuous integration and release packaging
- Time for milestones in [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md)

No telemetry, no paid unlocks inside the app — support stays voluntary.
